Global Temperature Change Across Malaysia

How a warming planet is changing life in Malaysia.

The Global Cause

Who Releases the Most Carbon Dioxide?

Carbon dioxide traps heat in the air.

China and the United States release nearly half of it.

Malaysia releases less than one percent. Yet heat ignores borders.

What Produces the Gases?

Power plants cause almost 1 in 3 tonnes.

Transport comes second, then factories and farms.

Malaysia's Emissions Keep Rising

Yearly emissions are nine times higher than in 1970.

Carbon dioxide is the biggest part.

Malaysia Heats Up

Malaysia Is Hot All Year

Temperatures barely change from month to month.

April and May are the warmest months.

With so little cool relief, extra heat is felt quickly.

Recent Years Run Hotter Than Usual

Each stem compares one year with the 1991–2020 average.

Blue years were cooler. Orange years were warmer.

Every year since 2015 has been warmer than usual.

Temperature by State

Weather Stations Across Malaysia

Bigger circles mean longer records.

Every station sits near sea level.

Every State Is Warmer

Blue dots show averages from 1951 to 1980.

Orange dots show averages from 1996 to 2025.

Each state warmed by 0.7 to 1.3°C.

Kuala Lumpur warmed the most.

What It Means

Malaysia's Rain Is Shifting

Each square is one month of rain.

Brown months were drier than usual. Purple months were wetter.

A 1998 drought left months far drier than usual.

Since 2020, months have been wetter on average.

Glaciers Are Disappearing

Melting ice raises sea levels worldwide.

Malaysia has long coasts and low coastal cities.

Heat Makes People Ill

Heat exhaustion is the most reported illness.

Heat stroke is rarer but far more dangerous.
